# Rockwell Study (1969)

The American Stock Exchange commissioned North American Rockwell Information Systems Company to examine securities-industry operations. Its 1969 report was titled *Securities Industry Overview, Final Report to the American Stock Exchange*.

The study proposed reducing physical certificate movement through a national clearing system linked to individual transfer agent depositories (TADs). Under the proposed structure, each issuer's transfer agent would maintain its shareholder register electronically and settle transfers by debiting and crediting positions on that register, rather than moving certificates. The Commission later described this as a theoretical model that had not then been implemented in any market.

The proposed network is distinct from the operating [TAD Depository Corporation (TADDC)](../history/taddc.md), which a 1976 Commission notice described as a possible bridge toward the TAD concept.
