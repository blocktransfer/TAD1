# Depository Trust Company (DTC) — 1973

The Depository Trust Company (DTC) was established in **1973, no later than May 11, 1973**, when it assumed the operations of Central Certificate Service, Inc. At that time, DTC was a wholly owned subsidiary of the New York Stock Exchange. May 11 is the documented operational-transfer date; it does not necessarily establish the earlier date on which DTC was legally formed.

DTC's [1973 annual report, p. 3](https://www.sechistorical.org/collection/papers/1970/1973_0101_DTCAR.pdf#page=5) documents the transfer and original ownership. The [New York State Banking Department's 2001 annual report](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2020/02/bd_areport_2001.pdf) independently records May 11, 1973 for DTC. For contemporary legislative context, see the [July 11–12, 1973 Senate hearings on clearing agencies and transfer agents](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/CHRG-93shrg98978O/CHRG-93shrg98978O), which occurred after the transfer.
