# Depository Trust Company

On 15 Sep 1972, drafts of DTC's articles of incorporation, bylaws, and rules were filed with the Commission for review, as well as with state and federal banking authorities. [BASIC, *Interindustry Teamwork* (1 Apr 1974), p. 42](https://www.sechistorical.org/collection/papers/1980/1984_0401_BasicTeamwork_1.pdf).

From Sep 1972 through early May 1973, CCS and Commission staff discussed and revised the proposed documents. They met on 29 Sep 1972; revised papers were refiled on 18 Oct 1972; the Commission publicly solicited comments on 8 Nov 1972; and ten comment letters were received. Further conferences and written submissions preceded the Commission's 8 May 1973 conditional clearance, subject to specified changes. [BASIC, *Interindustry Teamwork*, pp. 41–42](https://www.sechistorical.org/collection/papers/1980/1984_0401_BasicTeamwork_1.pdf).

Contemporary congressional testimony states that, after the New York Superintendent of Banks and Banking Board approved DTC's organization certificate, the Depository Trust Company (DTC) took over the assets and operations of Central Certificate Service, Inc. on 11 May 1973. The same testimony states that DTC's application for Federal Reserve System membership was approved on 19 Apr 1973 and became effective on 27 Jun 1973.

William Dentzer testified that CCS became “The Depository Trust Company” on 11 May 1973; he also reported Federal Reserve membership approval and that the SEC had virtually approved DTC's rules package. DTC was initially a wholly owned subsidiary of the New York Stock Exchange.

The [11–12 Jul 1973 Senate hearings on clearing agencies and transfer agents, p. 209](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-93shrg98978O/pdf/CHRG-93shrg98978O.pdf#page=209) document the organization-certificate approval, operational takeover and Federal Reserve membership milestones. The [other Senate testimony](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/CHRG-93shrg98978O/CHRG-93shrg98978O) describes DTC as having been known by that name since 11 May 1973.


DTC's [1973 annual report, p. 3 (PDF p. 5)](https://www.sechistorical.org/collection/papers/1970/1973_0101_DTCAR.pdf#page=5) discusses the operational transition and original NYSE ownership.

The [New York State Banking Department's 2001 annual report, Schedule B, Part 2, p. 4 of 14 (PDF pp. 118 and 133)](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2020/02/bd_areport_2001.pdf#page=118) independently lists 11 May 1973 as DTC's "Date of Charter or License." Because the column combines two different regulatory events, that listing does not, by itself, establish a distinct legal incorporation date.

DTC's [1973 annual report](https://www.sechistorical.org/collection/papers/1970/1973_0101_DTCAR.pdf#page=18) documents the transfer and original ownership. The [New York State Banking Department's 2001 annual report](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2020/02/bd_areport_2001.pdf) independently records 11 May 1973 for DTC. For contemporary legislative context, see the [11–12 Jul 1973 Senate hearings](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/CHRG-93shrg98978O/CHRG-93shrg98978O), which occurred after the transfer.

CCS ownership transfer to Series A preferred shares came on 4 Nov 1999.
