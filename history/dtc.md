# Depository Trust Company (DTC) — 1973

The Depository Trust Company (DTC) was established in **1973, no later than May 11, 1973**, when it assumed the operations of Central Certificate Service, Inc. At that time, DTC was a wholly owned subsidiary of the New York Stock Exchange. May 11 is the documented operational-transfer date. The New York State Banking Department also identifies it as DTC's **"Date of Charter or License"**; because that heading covers both charters and licenses, this record does not by itself establish DTC's separate legal incorporation date.

DTC's [1973 annual report, p. 3 (PDF p. 5)](https://www.sechistorical.org/collection/papers/1970/1973_0101_DTCAR.pdf#page=5) documents the transfer and original ownership. The [New York State Banking Department's 2001 annual report, Schedule B, Part 2, p. 4 of 14 (PDF pp. 118 and 133)](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2020/02/bd_areport_2001.pdf#page=118) lists May 11, 1973 under the "Date of Charter or License" column for DTC. For contemporary legislative context, see the [July 11–12, 1973 Senate hearings on clearing agencies and transfer agents](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/CHRG-93shrg98978O/CHRG-93shrg98978O), which occurred after the transfer.
