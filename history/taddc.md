# TAD Depository Corporation (TADDC)

TAD Depository Corporation (TADDC) was a securities depository distinct from the proposed network of individual transfer agent depositories (TADs). The Commission included TADDC among the clearing agencies registered as of June 30, 1976.

In a November 3, 1976 notice concerning the proposed National Securities Clearing Corporation (NSCC), the Commission described TADDC as intending to serve as a bridge to implementing the broader TAD concept. At that time, TADDC already had an interface with National Clearing Corporation (NCC), through which NCC participants could make or receive securities deliveries. The proposed NSCC arrangement was not yet described in detail.

TADDC's actual depository activity should not be conflated with implementation of the full, decentralized TAD system proposed in the [1969 Rockwell Study](../studies/rockwell.md).
